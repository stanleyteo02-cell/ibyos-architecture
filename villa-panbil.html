Case Study

Villa Panbil

A seaside villa composition in Batam defined by layered indoor-outdoor spaces, material warmth and a quiet sense of luxury.

A seaside villa with layered character.

Villa Panbil is composed as a series of intimate volumes that step down toward the coast, offering sheltered terraces and generous views across the functionally elegant plan.

The villa balances calm materiality with expressive geometry, using white plaster walls, textured wood tones and natural stone surfaces to create a refined yet comfortable coastal retreat.

Layered indoor-outdoor flow

A sequence of terraces, gardens and covered loggias ensures a strong connection between interior spaces and the coastal landscape.

Warm material palette

Timber, stone and soft plaster finishes combine into a cohesive palette that feels intimate and refined.

Private retreat planning

Bedrooms and living areas are arranged for privacy, comfort and a quiet sense of luxury in every room.

Coastal atmosphere

The villa design invokes a relaxed coastal elegance while maintaining strong architectural presence and detail.
